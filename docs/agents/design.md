# Approved product design

Read this before changing layout, motion, gestures, sheets, swipes, reordering,
navigation-detail behavior, or any other interaction contract.

`AGENTS.md` owns the standing rule against replacing the visual language. This
file owns the palette, the reference viewports, and the interaction contracts
built on them.

## References, viewports, and palette

The approved visual references are stored in `docs/design/`:

- `budget-responsive.png`: primary budget and responsive layout.
- `brand-system.png`: wordmark and app icon.
- `transactions.png`: transaction list, editing, and split flows.
- `auth-income-organizer.png`: sign-in, income, month copying, and organization.

Important responsive reference viewports are 390 x 844 for mobile and
1440 x 1000 for desktop.

The visual system is deliberately iOS-like and restrained: true-white surfaces,
charcoal text, and cool-gray dividers; cornflower blue `#1769E0` as the primary
action color; and mint `#55D49B`, sky `#9FC0FF`, yellow `#FFD977`, coral
`#FF7E83`, and lilac `#B6A6FF` as semantic accents. Pastel category medallions,
tactile sheets, rounded cards, lightweight CSS/SVG charts, a mobile bottom
navigation, and bottom-sheet interactions carry the rest. Desktop uses a slim
left navigation, a primary budget column, and a summary/activity rail.
Interactive targets are at least 44 px, with safe-area padding, keyboard focus
management, accessible status announcements, and reduced-motion support.

## Interaction contracts

- Category headers expose an edit menu; budget-item rows alone use a deliberately leftward swipe to reveal Delete. Keep the inactive swipe action fully transparent so fast vertical scrolling cannot flash its red layer.
- The Budget page defaults its amount display to Available unless the per-device Settings preference selects Planned. Switching Planned/Available on the Budget page remains temporary and must not resize rows. A line-item progress bar represents the share of its starting available balance still remaining: it is full before any net spending and shrinks toward empty as spending consumes the balance, including carried-in funds and refunds. A negative balance replaces the regular fill with a coral striped warning bar and an accessible over-budget amount. Reordering starts after a 350 ms long-press on the category header or item row; movement beyond 8 px before activation must cancel so vertical scrolling and item swipe-delete remain reliable. A visually hidden keyboard control must continue to support Arrow Up/Arrow Down reordering. Use a transition-free rendered drag copy inside a neutral compositor shell plus animated list reflow. Never move the source clone directly with inherited row/header transitions because that creates compositor ghosting. During a pointer drag, keep the real DOM order fixed, translate the faded placeholder into the current target slot, and move neighboring rows with interruptible transforms; commit the React/server order once on drop. Edge auto-scroll is frame-based with gradual acceleration/deceleration and continues while the pointer is held near an edge. Item reordering remains within its current category. Summary and budget progress entrance animations are one-shot and must not replay when a drag preview is created or a list order is committed.
- Budget-item details are URL-backed and use an iOS-style navigation push below 760 px. The detail page slides in from the right while the mobile header, Budget content, and bottom navigation parallax left; Back, browser history, refresh, and an app-controlled left-edge swipe all preserve navigation-stack semantics. The global left-edge guard must leave this custom swipe available from x=0 while suppressing Safari's cancelable native history gesture. Disable the underlying detail swipe while any child sheet is visible or exiting so add/edit transaction interactions cannot pop the line-item route. Keep the Budget back control and editable item title in a fixed detail header while only the detail body scrolls, led by a prominent remaining-this-month summary. A floating blue plus action remains at the bottom right and opens the add-transaction sheet with the current item preselected. Item-scoped add and edit transaction sheets are full-screen below 760 px, omit the mobile close control and grabber, and retain the header Add/Save action; at 760 px and above, they use the shared centered modal treatment. Global transaction sheets retain their established standard presentation.
- The mobile bottom navigation is a floating Liquid Glass-style capsule, inset from the screen edges and lifted above the home indicator, rather than an edge-attached bar. Safari exposes no native glass or backdrop refraction to web content, so the material is layered by hand: a lightly tinted translucent fill, a light `backdrop-filter` blur with saturation so content stays visible through the bar, a top specular sheen, and a light-catching gradient rim stroked along the continuous outline (a masked CSS rim until that outline is measured), with an opaque fallback where `backdrop-filter` is unsupported. The glass lives on its own `.bottom-nav-surface` layer rather than on the nav: the continuous-corner clip-path makes its element a backdrop root, so on the nav itself it would blank the blur. Write only the unprefixed `backdrop-filter` and `mask`: Tailwind's Lightning CSS generates the `-webkit-` forms, and a hand-written prefixed declaration after the standard one replaces it in the output, leaving Chromium with no blur at all. A single glass lens pill marks the active tab and slides between tabs on a spring curve with slight overshoot, squash-stretching as it travels. It must not stretch on first render, only on a tab change. Pressing the active tab swells the lens; tab icons never scale on press. The bar's inner width snaps to a multiple of 4 and its left edge to a whole pixel with CSS `round()`, so every tab edge and lens stop lands on a whole pixel; a half-pixel split lets the lens and tab layers round in different directions, visibly off-center on a phone. Tabs are not link-draggable, and the lens and tabs keep permanent compositing layers (`will-change: transform`) so icons do not re-snap by half a pixel while the lens animates. Reduced motion removes the slide and stretch. The bar and lens use continuous corners (below), and the bar waits offscreen until its shape is measured, then slides up on load; only `transform` animates, because opacity below 1 would make the nav a backdrop root and flash the glass clear. `--bottom-nav-height` is the capsule's full footprint including its bottom offset; content padding, scroll padding, the toast offset, and drag edge auto-scroll all derive from it or from the nav's measured top.
- Corners are continuous (curvature-smoothed) with one curve drawn two ways, set by three tokens in `tokens.css`: `--corner-superellipse` (1.8; higher is smoother and squarer), `--corner-extent` (1.55; how far past the authored radius the curve reaches, higher is rounder), and `--capsule-smoothing` (0.3) for pill ends. Apple's own corner is about superellipse 1.38 at extent 1.24, which at this app's sizes is within a fraction of a pixel of a plain circle, so the app deliberately runs more continuous than that. Where the browser draws `corner-shape` natively (Chromium today), one `@supports` rule gives every element the superellipse and sets `--corner-radius-scale` to the extent; every rectangular `border-radius` is therefore authored as `calc(Npx * var(--corner-radius-scale))`, and new ones must be too, while circles (`50%`) and pills (`999px`) stay literal. Elsewhere the scale stays 1 and corners are plain circles. On iOS today, continuous corners come from `src/components/ui/continuous-corners/`: `useContinuousCorners` for the tab bar and lens, the Budget and Income summary cards, and every sheet, and `ContinuousControls`, mounted once in `providers.tsx`, which attaches to every element matching its button selector list (primary and soft buttons, the Expense/Income segmented control and its thumb, filter tabs, the icon, tone, and organizer action buttons, the sheet header submit, the month picker control and its month cells, and the toast action) as they enter the DOM. Add a class to that list when a new button style needs continuous corners. Both apply the same superellipse and extent (their elements keep a scale of 1), or a capsule end when the radius fills a side. The element paints the largest circular radius that contains the curve and its clip-path removes only the slivers between that circle and the curve, so shadows, focus rings, and contents keep painting normally. A CSS border stays and is also stroked along the curve as a background layer, re-read whenever a state attribute, hover, or border transition changes it. That layer is `!important`, because component state rules restyle with the `background` shorthand, which would reset it at any specificity. The border color must be opaque so the two do not double up, and a bordered continuous element must use `background-color` rather than a background image. The mechanics, all in `src/components/ui/continuous-corners/`: `geometry.ts` first mirrors CSS by shrinking radii that overflow a side together by one factor, then scales each by the extent. A corner whose scaled radius fills a whole side becomes a capsule end, whose curvature ramps linearly from zero (a clothoid) through the `--capsule-smoothing` share of its 90° turn along the longer edge and then holds as a circular arc; every other corner is the `superellipse(K)` curve with exponent 2^K, exactly what `corner-shape` draws. `attach.ts` sizes from the ResizeObserver border-box size, which is fractional and ignores transforms: `offsetWidth` rounds, and the half-pixel error was visible at the tab bar's 4 px gaps. `useContinuousCorners` returns a callback ref, so an element that remounts (the lens replays its stretch by remounting) is re-attached, and `ContinuousControls` skips elements a hook already manages. `ContinuousStroke` draws a gradient rim inside the outline for surfaces that have no CSS border, which is how the tab bar and lens get their light-catching edges. A decorative shape attached this way must be a real element, not a pseudo-element, which is why the segmented control's thumb is a `<span>`. The clip applies to the element's whole subtree, so anything overhanging a continuous element's corner must be its sibling, not its child: the Transactions filter count badge sits beside the filter button in a positioned wrapper for that reason. A radius scaled past half an element's side becomes a circle or pill end, and one that nearly reaches it leaves almost no straight edge: a small square control reads as a circle. Keep the authored radius times the extent to about 85% of half the element's shorter side, which is why the 44 px filter button uses 12 px rather than 14 px. Circles and pills keep `corner-shape: round`: add any new one to the opt-out list in `tokens.css`, or the native superellipse will square it off.
- The detail header title collapses with the scroll, never on a timer. The expanded title wraps and is left aligned; as it scales toward the compact bar its wrapped remainder dissolves, the single-line layout takes over at 65 % of the collapse, and the text that line could not show reveals across the remaining scroll so the motion reverses when scrolling back up. Truncate the compact title against its drawn width rather than its laid-out width, keep it centered in the bar, and keep the visible text continuous across the layout swap. Register any custom property used for these fades with `@property`; unregistered custom properties and CSS gradients animate discretely and snap.
- The Transactions page keeps its All, Expenses, and Income type pills visible and applies those inline choices immediately. Its sliders control opens a full filter sheet with transaction type, budget item, and split-status controls. Sheet changes are drafts until Apply filters is pressed; closing the sheet discards them, and the sheet's Clear filters action resets only the drafts. Applied filters give the sliders control a distinct blue treatment and active-count badge. While filters are applied, expose an outside Clear action immediately after the Income pill in the same left-aligned group, never pushed to the far edge of the row. That action resets the applied type, item, and split filters without clearing search. A populated search field exposes an accessible clear control that empties the query without returning focus to the input.
- All sheets share the same interaction contract: transparent/non-dimming overlay, 0.6 s `cubic-bezier(0.29, 1, 0.29, 1)` entrance, 0.45 s `cubic-bezier(0.4, 1, 0.4, 1)` exit, and mobile downward drag-to-dismiss with distance, velocity, and projected-distance thresholds. Keep the grabber, title, and close control fixed while only `.sheet-body` scrolls; prevent horizontal sheet overflow. Desktop sheets use the same timing as centered modals without drag dismissal.
- Sheets focus their content container on open instead of drawing a focus ring
  around the close control. When a pointer-opened sheet closes, restore focus
  without a visible ring; keyboard-opened sheets must preserve visible keyboard
  focus on restoration.
- The mobile PWA intentionally disables page zoom, text selection, touch callouts, document-level pull-to-refresh, and cancelable Safari history gestures beginning within the leftmost 20 px on any route. The global touch guard must remain capture-phase and non-passive for touchstart and touchmove, claim non-control edge touches immediately, preserve normal control taps, and prevent every move that begins inside the edge strip regardless of direction. Mount it once above all routes. `.app-content` is the vertical overscroll surface for every route, continues behind the floating glass bottom navigation, and must retain enough bottom padding/scroll padding to expose the final content above that navigation. The top header remains opaque white, and route content enters over 0.65 s with the established blur-to-sharp motion. Changing the month with the header arrows replaces that motion with a two-layer horizontal slide on the shared `--page-slide-motion` timing, which reuses the navigation-detail push curve and which both layers must read from so they cannot drift apart: the outgoing month slides off one edge while the incoming month slides in from the other, matched 1:1 so the two read as one moving surface. Forward months enter from the right and earlier months from the left. The outgoing layer is a static DOM snapshot cloned when the arrow is pressed, so it must stay visually frozen — every animation and transition inside it is suppressed — and it is clipped to the content area rather than crossing the desktop rail. A month change with no matching snapshot, including the month picker and browser history, keeps the blur-to-sharp entrance instead of sliding half of it. Settings and Organize suppress the entrance animation entirely for month changes, however the month was chosen, and every tab change keeps the blur-to-sharp motion.
- The Budget page adds a touch pull-to-refresh above `.app-content`; no other route has one. The gesture claims only a touch that starts at the top of the scroller, travels downward past 12 px, and is more vertical than horizontal, and it never starts while a sheet, a pushed detail, or a list drag is active, so swipe-to-delete, hold-to-drag, and ordinary scrolling keep priority. Pulling translates the scroll container and a fixed 58 px indicator band that otherwise parks beneath the opaque header, so the dial emerges from under the header while the band's bottom edge stays flush with the content. Distance is rubber-banded toward a 148 px maximum, the dial's arc completes at the 62 px release threshold, and release either settles back or parks the band at 58 px while the month refetches. The spinner holds for at least 540 ms and at most 5 s, and the outcome is announced in a polite live region rather than a toast. The scroll container is only transformed while the gesture owns it, so nothing positions against it at rest. Reduced motion keeps the gesture and drops the frame interpolation and the spin.
- A slim desktop left navigation, primary budget column, and summary/activity rail.
- At least 44 px interactive targets, plus safe-area padding, keyboard focus management, accessible status announcements, and reduced-motion support.

Do not replace the established brand or visual language with a generic dashboard theme. Extend existing primitives and tokens first.
